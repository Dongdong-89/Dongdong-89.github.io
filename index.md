# Chunhua Dong, PhD

I am a **Computer Scientist, Researcher, and Adjunct Professor** specializing in the intersection of Deep Learning, Computer Vision, and Applied Data Science. My work focuses on building robust machine learning and deep learning pipelines to solve complex, high-dimensional data challenges across **Agricultural AI, Bioinformatics, and Advanced Biometrics**.

- I am currently working as an Adjunct Professor and Postdoctoral Scientist at **Fort Valley State University**.
- I am the primary instructor for CSCI 4303 (Advanced Python), CSCI 1301 (Java), and CSCI 1153 (Intro to Computer).
-  My research applies Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Advanced Analytics to remote sensing, agricultural, genomic, and medical datasets.

##  Featured Projects & Research Implementation

###  MethylC-Analyzer
A command-line software tool utilizing Python and statistical modeling designed to process and interpret high-resolution genomic data and DNA methylation patterns.

###  Predictive Crop Modeling
Implementation of optimized ensemble machine learning models to predict peanut and pecan crop yields by analyzing climate impact factors and food safety metrics in Central Georgia agriculture.

###  Target Detection Project
Advanced machine learning pipelines utilizing feature extraction and sparse representation to accurately classify and detect targets within complex spatial-spectral hyperspectral datasets[cite: 2].

###  Biometric Recognition Project
A deep face recognition architecture designed for cross-domain matching between thermal and visible spectra, leveraging TensorFlow, PyTorch, and GANs to bridge domain gaps and extract robust facial features.
